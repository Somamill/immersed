# Immersed Store edition

## Platform
Native iOS/iPadOS, with a Mac Catalyst build under validation. Expo SDK 58, TypeScript, local SQLite and Apple-supported video playback.

## Users and purpose
Japanese learners watching imported videos. The owner confirmed the primary flow: watch and tap subtitle words. Transcripts and saved vocabulary support that flow.

## Capabilities and constraints
User-owned or authorized unprotected media; imported UTF-8 SRT/VTT subtitles; offline exact-form JMdict lookup; local saved words. No third-party extraction, downloaders, DRM bypass, screen capture, hosted language service, accounts, or advertising. Preserve the existing full community edition in the repository. Apple approval and device acceptance are release gates.

## Brand commitments
Use the established dot. brand kit, Immersed purple accent, shared typography and interaction rules. Preserve Immersed’s focus on Japanese media. Do not replace the suite identity with a new visual theme.
