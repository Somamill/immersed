# Voice and copy

Use calm, direct language and sentence case. Name an action with a verb: Save article, Open book, Connect Goodreads. State errors plainly and offer a useful next action. Use “Could not connect. Check the address and try again.” rather than unexplained error codes.

Shared navigation terms: Library, Search, Settings, About, Connections. Keep task-specific language: read. uses reading and highlights; listen. uses audiobook discovery and downloads; Immersed uses subtitles, vocabulary, and immersion.

Product descriptions:
- read. — Books, articles, and highlights.
- listen. — Find your next audiobook.
- Immersed — Japanese worth watching.

Do not describe discovery or download handoff as audiobook playback. Do not claim sync, offline support, privacy properties, or platform availability beyond what the app actually implements.

Use “Open read.”, “Open listen.”, and “Open Immersed” for companion actions. Offer a website link when the companion app is not installed. A shared suite name does not imply a shared account or automatic data sharing.
